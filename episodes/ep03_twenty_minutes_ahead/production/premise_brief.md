# EPISODE 3 — "TWENTY MINUTES AHEAD"
## Final Premise Brief · "There Is No Homeless" Ch.1 · ~20 min

---

## PREMISE (one paragraph)

The morning after Obi's collapse, John vanishes — not flaking, but misfired care: at the hospital he heard "we don't know if you're contagious," and the man who spent all his money on bandages walked out AMA to remove himself from the only people who touch him. Nobody searching knows this yet. Obi, wrecked by Bitter Dividend and needing to be useful to deserve love, insists on leading a search he physically cannot lead; Hunter helps without forgiving; Seth/Mark holds the impossible clue — a P. Terry's receipt and hospital wristband both stamped twenty minutes in the future, Integrity 100% — plus a hard drive of footage shot "just for the drama." The search runs the real corridor west-to-east and back — rumor telegraph, Norm's electrical-box camp at Sterzing, a stonewalling hospital — then turns upstream into untouched Greenbelt territory: Gus Fruh, Twin Falls, Sculpture Falls, and finally a seep pool below the Hill of Life, Wiley guiding by land because he knows what woods-longing looks like, TK poling a raft up a bone-dry creek because the river gets a vote even when it abstains. The receipt's twenty-minute lead half-works exactly once and half-fails exactly once — Bellweather stays predict/remember/cause-ambiguous, glimpsed a single time descaling a kettle on the creek bank ("The kettle doesn't know either") — but what actually finds John is Mark's footage: the exploitation reel contains the knowledge of a man ("soaked in bleach, baby") that only real attention could have recorded. The MRSA truth is dramatized at the pool — treatable, doxycycline works, precautions suffice, John is not poison. Magic can be twenty minutes ahead of a person; love turns out to be the better predictive engine. As Bellweather puts it, in the episode's governing aphorism: **"Springs don't predict rain. They live downstream of it."**

---

## EMOTIONAL SPINE

Mirrored care deepens by inverting direction. Ep2's care was bodies carrying bodies; Ep3's care is **knowledge carrying a body** — and the hardest carry is making John accept it. Each searcher metabolizes their Ep2 mistake into its next lesson:

- **OBI** (mistook intelligence for understanding) now distrusts his own cleverness and does the unthinkable: asks Hunter to check his reasoning — accepting help while limited, wrong, and in pain, neck feathers down. His wounded pride still can't credit Mark directly; the closest he gets is the register of **"The sky drew it. Mark held the pen adjacent."**
- **HUNTER** (mistook information for truth) refuses to act on the receipt until it is ground-truthed. He comes on the search without granting absolution — help and forgiveness explicitly decoupled, the incomplete apology left incomplete. The conflict stays alive into future episodes.
- **SETH/MARK** (tolerated exploitation "just for the footage") must scrub his own footage to find John — forced to watch himself setting the camera down too late, over and over. Then the reel he's ashamed of becomes the act of witness that saves. **His camera arc is a three-step escalation across acts: Act 1 he films everything; Act 2 he half-films, camera drifting down mid-take; Act 3 he leaves it in the truck for the hospital and the pool** — the one stretch of the story that exists in-world but not in his footage. One uncanny fingerprint: during the footage scrub, a bell rings twenty minutes before he understands why, and he sets the camera down *before* the moment for the first time in his life.
- **JOHN**, who accepted uncertainty best, is the one who cannot accept care: his self-abuse theory turned inward means exile-as-protection. The climax is Mark handing John's own line back to him — "maybe you need to care about yourself more" — while Obi, in pain, takes John's bandaged hand knowing exactly what it might cost. Being found = finally letting people abuse you less than you abuse yourself.
- **WILEY** gets one gospel wobble, quiet and unresolved: **"I go to the woods to hear myself, man. He went to stop."**

Bitter Dividend's clay weather permits one tiny warmth, and this is where it's spent: a single shaft of warm rain on the carry home. No cure. Just permission. (Invariant 7/8 honored: small, paid-for, cut before euphoria overclaims.)

## COMEDY ENGINES

Demetri-deadpan inversion, warm-blooded: the supernatural is boring logistics, the mundane is cosmic. Warm rule: every laugh is someone loving someone badly on purpose.

1. **The Twenty-Minute Log** (running gag, scenes 3–7): Seth keeps an official ledger of prophecy on a greasy P. Terry's bag — *"Bell. ...Twenty minutes. Norm sneezed. Circle it."* The bag degrades physically as the data gets more portentous; by the stakeout it is translucent with grease and treated like the Zapruder film.
2. **Flat skepticism vs. the stamp**: Seth timestamps a receipt from the future with "they're not that interesting, dude," then conducts a full Socratic inquest into Walgreens-versus-CVS pharmacy loyalty. Payoff at the hospital pharmacy window: a pharmacist who **can neither confirm nor deny that a Walgreens exists.**
3. **Institutional-stonewall farce** (scene 5): "Are you family?" / "I'm his... documentarian." / "...Sir." Plus the A.T.T. phone-tree — "WE IMPROVE WHAT YOU MEANT" — whose hold music satirically improves the crew's own messages back at them.
4. **The rumor telegraph**: a contradiction machine (he's hospitalized / he got a maintenance job / he went to Round Rock, which everyone agrees is worse than missing), paying off John's canon line when found: "you guys care enough about me to make up a fucking rumor?"
5. **The stakeout**: five people staring at an empty picnic table, Seth narrating "any second now" for eleven minutes, the greasy bag consulted like scripture.
6. **TK's dry-creek raft**: joyfully poling up a waterless creek bed. "River gets a vote." "There's no river, man." "Unanimous, then." And on the missing water: **"the road left too."** Wiley's rebuttal: **"The creek's not gone, man. It's just introverted."**
7. **Obi's wounded pride**: insults funny and specific while his body refuses to back them up; credit-denial in the "pen adjacent" register.
8. **John, found**: gallows engine at full throttle — "morphine and monkey pox," tangent-stacking into a Drew Carey bit mid-tears, and **reciting his allergy list to a resident in full Drew Carey cadence** (amoxicillin, erythromycin, clindamycin — a tight three-beat structure with a Buzz Beer tag).

## ACT SHAPE — 13 scenes + post-credit tag (~20 min)

### ACT 1 — THE ABSENCE (S1–S4, ~6 min)

- **S1 · Cold open.** Dawn at Wiley's woods camp. Obi wakes wrecked. John gone — but half his bandages left folded for Obi's strap-wounds, and beside them **a Tupperware container with a single muffin in it** (plant; nobody comments beyond Wiley: "he left you breakfast, man"). Care even in leaving. *how-i-wish* under the empty space. Title card.
- **S2.** Seth arrives with receipt + wristband. Obi insists on leading, first refuses help. Seth's flat skepticism vs. the stamp that bothers him. The P. Terry's bag enters as evidence container; **the Twenty-Minute Log begins.** Mark films everything (camera arc step 1).
- **S3.** Rumor-telegraph montage down the corridor. Contradiction comedy; Round Rock consensus. Log entries accumulate. Act-break ignition on *starts.*
- **S4.** Norm's camp at the Sterzing electrical box. Infection-source thread touched, not resolved. One Sadi-finger beat silences Truth exactly once. The clue that reframes everything: John was here **asking Norm how HE got treated.** He's not fleeing; he's questing alone. ("Norm sneezed. Circle it.")

### ACT 2 — THE THEORIES (S5–S9, ~8 min)

- **S5 · Hospital stonewall.** The full bureaucracy kit: "documentarian" / "...Sir.", the A.T.T. phone-tree and improved-hold-music satire, the Walgreens-agnostic pharmacist. *hey-joe* against the privacy desk. **Mark leaves the camera in the truck** (camera arc step 3 previewed early — this scene exists in-world but not in his footage). Midpoint turn: John checked in, heard "we don't know if you're contagious," walked out AMA. His motive lands.
- **S6.** Obi asks Hunter for help. Help granted, forgiveness withheld. The apology stays incomplete and the scene is better for it.
- **S7 · The twenty-minute stakeout.** The theory half-works (fresh traces, missed him by minutes) and half-fails, exactly once each — Bellweather ambiguity locked. The Log's finest and stupidest hour. On the walk out, **the single Bellweather cameo**: no shop, no cup, descaling a kettle on the creek bank. Obi asks if the kettle knew they'd miss him. "The kettle doesn't know either." She never offers tea.
- **S8 · The footage scrub.** Night. *fog*, with one quoted instrumental phrase of *puddle* inside it (Ep1 callback — Mark's low point revisited, not repeated). Mark half-films himself watching (camera arc step 2), watches his own absences, camera-down-too-late on loop. **A bell rings, faint, source-less. He sets the camera down. Twenty minutes later** he finds the tell: the bleach foot-soak riff — "soaked in bleach, baby" — means an upstream spring. Uncanny fingerprint on his redemption, unexplained.
- **S9 · Into new territory.** The named upstream route by land with Wiley — **Gus Fruh's graffiti wall, a false-find at Twin Falls, Sculpture Falls** — reluctant thesis evidence again ("I go to the woods to hear myself, man. He went to stop."). TK poles the raft up the bone-dry creek bed ("the road left too"). Documentary lenses on limestone and live oak, dry-mineral soundscape, no skyline, no rainforest loops. Reference frames from user footage + legal production photos before environment lock.

### ACT 3 — THE FINDING (S10–S13, ~6 min)

- **S10.** John at the **seep pool below the Hill of Life**, feet in bleach-white spring water, gallows engine at full throttle. The rumor line. His contagion confession — exile-as-protection, out loud at last.
- **S11 · The climax of touch.** Obi crosses the distance and takes John's bandaged hand knowing what it might cost. MRSA truth dramatized: treatable, doxycycline works, precautions suffice, not poison — delivered via **John reciting his allergy list to the on-call resident like a Drew Carey bit**, laughing and crying in the same take. Mark returns John's own line. **The Tupperware surfaces from Obi's pack — the muffin, carried the whole search, is by now genuinely dry** — and *my-muffin-is-dry* lands as a prop payoff, not just a needle-drop. Camera stays in the truck.
- **S12 · The carry home.** The group carries John as John carried Obi. Obi lets Hunter steady him on the trail. Obi's near-credit to Mark: "The sky drew it. Mark held the pen adjacent." One tiny clay-weather warmth spent: thirty seconds of warm rain.
- **S13 · Coda / final pre-credits image.** Hospital room, evening. **John's IV bag drips in perfect 4/4.** Mark notices. Mark does not film it. A bell rings somewhere, faint. The weather is one degree kinder. No answers about Bellweather.

### POST-CREDIT TAG (Ep2 pattern honored)
Off Azie Morton behind Barton Springs Pool's south side: first-ever glimpse of the **Monkey Tree**. Someone has hung a single small bell in its branches. No wind. It rings once. Then a plain on-screen fact card, documentary-style: *hydrologists trace Barton Springs' water with dye — rain that falls miles away surfaces here days later.* One true thing under the one mysterious thing. Springs don't predict rain; they live downstream of it. Seeds the drum-circle venue and the bell mythology in one real, verifiable location.
